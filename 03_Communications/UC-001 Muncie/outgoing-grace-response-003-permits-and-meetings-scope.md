## Immediate Classification

This is the follow-up correspondence after the 2026-07-20 reduced-AOR call (`incoming-uhg-review-request-002-reduced-aor-call.md`). ## Key New Reality Signals

1. **Permitting is included in the fee, not a separate paid line item** — both the permit application process and responding to review comments are covered, and are considered part of CA hours allowance. This explains why Preconstruction/Bidding/Permitting Phase lines were zeroed in both projects' 2026-07-22 matrices: that work didn't disappear, it moved into Construction Development / Construction Phase pricing, consistent with those lines' large increases (Muncie Construction Development Phase +$5,555; Russiaville +$4,220).
2. **Permit fees themselves are reimbursable**, separate from the design fee — a real Reimbursable Expenses detail not previously captured for either project.
3. **Meeting assumptions, both projects together:**
   - One existing-conditions site survey meeting per site, both sites visited the same day (single combined trip) — this is the Site Due Diligence line item's actual scope.
   - Schematic Design meeting: virtual.
   - Weekly check-in meetings: virtual, as needed.
   - During construction: three site visits per site, both sites combined into the same-day trips (three combined trips total, not six separate ones) — this is what the Construction Phase / Construction Administration line items are actually pricing.

## Immediate Pii Meaning

This closes a real interpretation gap that the SO matrices alone couldn't answer: why Permitting/Bidding fees zeroed out while Construction Development Phase fees rose sharply was previously unexplained pattern-matching (see `so-matrix-fee-basis-analysis-muncie-2026-07-22-update.md` / `-russiaville-2026-07-22-update.md`). It also gives concrete, billable meeting/site-visit scope that neither matrix nor either proposal document stated explicitly.
