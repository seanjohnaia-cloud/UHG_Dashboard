## Current Understanding

Muncie Family Physicians is a small remodel clinic project under the UHG master agreement, classified Schedule F / Clinic Project Size (0-2,500 SF). The engagement began as a request to hammer out small-project fees — no formal Service Order was prepared at intake, and Grace's proposal covered architectural services only, with MEP explicitly excluded for now (understood likely to be needed later).

A fee was first proposed at $27,950 (per-Contract baseline $34,760, fixed by classification). Following an internal UHG review and a call on 2026-07-20, UHG requested a reduced Architect-of-Record scope — moving away from a full Design Development phase and advancing more directly toward a 90% Construction Documents set. Grace worked this scope reduction collaboratively with UHG's Design Experience team and re-priced the deliverable matrix accordingly, landing on a custom fee of **$23,650** as of 2026-07-22. This figure is not yet confirmed as accepted by UHG.

Separately, UHG's Shannon Dupras confirmed (2026-07-23/26) that permit application work and responding to permit review comments are included in the fee, folded into the Construction Administration hours allowance — permit fees themselves bill as a reimbursable expense. Meeting assumptions are also confirmed: one combined existing-conditions site survey trip covering both Muncie and Russiaville, a virtual Schematic Design meeting, weekly virtual check-ins, and three combined site visits during construction.

## What This Explains

The 2026-07-22 matrix's pattern — Design Development, Permitting, and Bidding phases all dropping to $0 while the Construction Development Phase line rose sharply — is now understood, not just observed: that work moved into CA hours rather than disappearing.
