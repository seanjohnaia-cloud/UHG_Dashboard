OWNER SEGMENT(S) RECEIVING SERVICES:

Enterprise Real Estate Services

PURPOSE AND GENERAL SCOPE OF SERVICES:

The Owner manages a large, complex real estate portfolio consisting of administrative, clinical, and OptumRx/ pharmacy fulfillment. Most projects completed by the Owner are tenant improvements with scopes ranging from new sites to remodeling and refreshing. Owner has well-defined programs, standards and design guidelines which form the basis of the design approach for most projects (the

“Program Guidelines”).

The Owner expects to deploy a delivery model that utilizes a party (the “Architect of Record”) to provide Design Services related to the implementation of the Owner’s Program Guidelines during standard project delivery. The Architect of Record will coordinate with Design Experience to understand and deploy the Owner’s Program Guidelines, processes, and procedures.

Architect will be responsible for the delivery of professional design services (the “Design Services”) to support the further development, maintenance and implementation of the Program Guidelines or as directed and approved by the

Owner or its Designated Representative (as defined in this SOW) in non-standard applications.

At the direction of the Owner, the Architect will provide management, oversight and execution for the detailed design services listed below.

Architect shall assign appropriate resources to oversee the management of the Owner’s account and the Owner’s projects. Resources assigned to support the Owner’s projects shall have extensive knowledge of the Architect processes and services and extensive knowledge of the Owner’s standards, design guidelines, project management processes, and administrative requirements.

The Architect shall assign personnel to support the Owner’s projects. Architect ‘s personnel shall be responsible for the day-to-day management of the Architect obligations as they related to the Owner’s projects.

Architect Services may include, but are not limited to, any or all the following phases: Project Initiation, Programming, Planning, Design (Preliminary Floor Plans, Design Development, Construction Documents) Procurement/Bidding, Construction Administration & Occupancy, Close Out and Post- Occupancy/Warranty Site Visit. Project will be categorized by the following types: New Sites, Remodels, Infrastructure projects and Restack/Refresh. See [Appendix A, Definitions] attached hereto for definitions of each project type. Projects may also have the following ancillary services: Additional Space Plans, Consultant Services and Coordination, and Field Measurements / Site Visits. Firm will also provide, host, or manage certain aspects of technology products and services to support Owner's design delivery and space information management functions and processes.
