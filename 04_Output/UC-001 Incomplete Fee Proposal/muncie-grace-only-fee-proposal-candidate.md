## 1. Request Summary

UHG requested a Grace architectural-services-only fee proposal for the Muncie Family Physicians small remodel scope. UHG clarified that MEP consultant pricing is not needed at this time and that a formal Service Order is not required today. The current purpose is to evaluate / hammer out small-project fees.

## 2. Scope Understanding

Based on the email request and Muncie plan notes, Grace understands the Muncie scope to include limited renovation work associated with Research / Room 2091 and adjacent imaging/x-ray planning coordination.

The understood scope includes:

- demo/reconfiguration of Room 2091 interior to support supply storage and an ambient drug study room;
- drywall/enclosure work and addition of a door on the south side;
- accommodation of two existing locked cabinets coming from Franklin, with final specifications to be provided by the customer;
- new storage shelving, including perimeter shelving and cabinets in the middle of the room;
- no wire shelving;
- printer/copy area with power/data;
- counter-height power raceway for charging;
- storage accommodation for equipment such as EKG and printer/copy equipment;
- LVT flooring;
- punch-door security at Room 2091 and Room 2092;
- x-ray / imaging planning coordination, including RSI suggested layout, relocation of the existing x-ray machine from the Carmel AHN site, and priority for direct adjacency between the dressing room and x-ray room.

Grace understands that some MEP coordination will likely be required as the project develops, but MEP consultant fees are not included in this current pricing exercise per UHG direction.
