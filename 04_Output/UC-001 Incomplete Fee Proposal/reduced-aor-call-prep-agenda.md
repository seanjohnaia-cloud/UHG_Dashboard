# Reduced AOR Scope Call Prep — Monday, July 20

## Purpose of Call

Discuss UHG's proposed reduced AOR approach for the small project fee proposals and clarify impacts to scope, schedule, and fees.

## UHG's Proposed Direction

```text
Reduced AOR scope focused primarily on site due diligence, followed by advancement directly to a 90% CD set for review and final comments.
```

## Authority / Governance Constraint

The user can help coordinate and prepare the response, but should not independently commit Grace to a reduced AOR scope or reduced fee because the user is on the UHG Admin Team, not the Clinical Projects team, is not the PIC, and cannot approve fee reductions without internal authorization.

Before any substantive commitment, Grace needs Clinical Projects team / PIC review and agreement on meeting posture.

## Grace's Call Objectives

1. Understand what UHG wants included in reduced AOR scope.
2. Identify which standard MSA deliverables UHG wants omitted or compressed.
3. Clarify whether direct-to-90% CD is acceptable given available information.
4. Identify risks/dependencies that could affect schedule or fee.
5. Determine whether the current proposal should be revised, reframed, or split into options.
6. Avoid committing to a scope or fee reduction until the proper team/PIC approves.

## Questions to Ask UHG

### Reduced AOR Scope

1. When you say "reduced AOR scope," which services do you expect Grace to retain?
2. Which MSA/Schedule F deliverables are you expecting to omit or combine?
3. Is the reduced scope intended for both Muncie and Russiaville, or should we discuss each project separately?

### Site Due Diligence

4. What level of site due diligence are you expecting before moving to 90% CD?
5. Are existing CAD/Revit backgrounds available and reliable?
6. Should Grace field-verify existing conditions, dimensions, and affected walls/rooms?
7. Should Grace perform any code/life-safety review before 90% CD?

### Direct-to-90% CD

8. What information will UHG provide before Grace advances to 90% CD?
9. Who will provide x-ray/vendor/security/IT/power/data requirements?
10. How many review/comment cycles should be included after the 90% CD set?
11. If major scope comments arise at 90% CD, should those be treated as additional services or included?

### Fee / Schedule

12. Are you looking for a reduced fee from the current proposal, or primarily a reframing of the scope basis?
13. Should Grace provide one reduced-AOR fee option or a comparison against the submitted proposal?
14. What response date do you need after the call?

## Grace Talking Points

- We can discuss a reduced AOR approach, but the scope must be clearly defined so fee and schedule align with responsibility.
- Moving directly to 90% CD may reduce front-end deliverables, but it increases dependency on complete owner/vendor information.
- For Muncie, x-ray/security/power/data issues may still require coordination even if MEP consultant fees are not included at this stage.
- If UHG wants reduced scope, Grace should identify what is excluded/deferred and how later changes will be handled.

## Possible Response Paths After Call

| Path | Description |
|---|---|
| Option A | Keep current proposal and clarify inclusions/exclusions. |
| Option B | Revise to reduced AOR site-due-diligence + 90% CD scope. |
| Option C | Provide side-by-side current MSA path vs reduced AOR path. |
| Option D | Provide reduced-AOR proposal with assumptions and additional-service triggers. |
