# Russiaville SO Matrix — 2026-07-22 Update

## Process (per Sean, 2026-07-26 dialogue — not inferred from the documents alone)

Both Muncie and Russiaville went through the same three-stage process:

1. Matrix built from contractual assumptions/requirements — the per-Contract column, presented as a **base fee to negotiate from**.
2. Scope requirements reduced **with the assistance of the client/owner's design-experience PM team** — a collaborative conversation, not a unilateral Grace cut.
3. **Custom scope pricing estimated** based on the newly understood, limited scope — this is the Proposed column in this version.

This resolves the open question left in the earlier Muncie update record (whether $23,650 was the new intended output, an interim position, or still moving): it is stage-3 custom pricing for the negotiated reduced scope, not drift or an unexplained revision.
