## Interpretation

Design Development Phase, Permitting, Bidding, and Preliminary Floor Plan all dropped to $0 — a full scope removal, not a discount — while Construction Development Phase nearly doubled. Read together, this is consistent with the already-recorded reduced-AOR direction: advancing directly from Schematic Design to a 90% CD set rather than running a full DD phase, with the CD-phase line item absorbing work that would otherwise have sat in DD/Permitting/Bidding.

Site Due Diligence and Schematic Design Documents moved by comparatively small amounts (+$330 / −$110) — consistent with normal proposal refinement rather than a scope-structure change.

## Process — resolved (per Sean, 2026-07-26 dialogue)

The question below was originally raised as unresolved from the documents alone. Sean has since confirmed the mechanism directly, and it applies to both Muncie and Russiaville (see `so-matrix-fee-basis-analysis-russiaville-2026-07-22-update.md` for the parallel Russiaville case): the matrix went through three stages — (1) built from contractual assumptions as a base fee to negotiate from, (2) scope reduced with the assistance of the client/owner's design-experience PM team, (3) custom scope pricing estimated for the newly limited scope. $23,650.00 is stage-3 output — the current negotiated custom price, not drift or an interim guess.

`readiness-assessment.md`'s "Remaining Deficiencies" and "Current Recommendation" sections still cite the $27,950.00 figure. That figure is now stale relative to this artifact and the confirmed process; whether/how to revise `readiness-assessment.md` itself remains Sean's call.
