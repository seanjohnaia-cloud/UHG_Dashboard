## Comparison to Muncie — a cleaner Required/Proposed pattern

Russiaville marks 8 of 14 lines Required (all of Schematic Design through Construction Phase), and every Required line has a nonzero Proposed value while every non-Required line is $0 — Required and Proposed line up exactly.

Muncie (both dates) does not follow this pattern: only 4 lines are marked Required, yet a 5th line (Schematic Design Documents) carries a nonzero Proposed fee despite not being marked Required, and in the 2026-07-22 version one Required line (Preliminary Floor Plan) drops to $0.00 Proposed. Required and "gets charged" are not the same field in either project, but Russiaville's usage is closer to matching than Muncie's.
