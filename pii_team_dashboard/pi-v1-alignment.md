## Alignment rules for this seed

1. **Pi v1 is the reference WikiLLM/PI model; UHG_Dashboard is the local project instance.**
   - Do not write into Pi v1 while designing the UHG seed.
   - Do not treat Pi v1 synthesis as automatically authoritative inside UHG; use it as the constitutional/pattern reference and cite local UHG records where possible.

2. **Dashboard status is not authority.**
   - Pii Team Dashboard renders project/gov state.
   - It must preserve source/evidence basis, truth state, scoped use, blocking effect, and authority requirement.

3. **PAC is Layer 0 / origin-intake.**
   - PAC captures what is known, approved, and structurally defined before startup.
   - Downstream validation or transformation may not overwrite PAC origin truth.

4. **Activation is Section A, not the whole startup system.**
   - The 14 gates are activation controls.
   - LCW Sections B-F still matter: responsibility, financial modeling, operational alignment, system setup, and design inputs.

5. **Generic LCD-W/LCW and UHG LCD/LCW are overlays, not PAC.**
   - Generic LCD-W/LCW is the likely Grace project-data workbook layer.
   - UHG LCD/LCW is a client-specific, contract/workflow-bound overlay.
   - UHG may teach Pii patterns, but UHG fields do not define Pii core without generalization.

6. **Field truth state travels with every important value.**
   - Candidate states: `provided`, `extracted`, `validated`, `assumed`, `missing`, `conflict`, `deferred`, `unknown`, `not_required`.
   - `not_required` is scoped to current output; it does not mean never required.
   - `extracted` is not `validated`.

7. **Centerline is deferred unless scope is reopened.**
   - Current seed may use a Centerline-style context handoff metaphor.
   - It must not assume live Centerline navigation, ownership, data model, or authority.

8. **Use Archive, not Quarantine, as the console function.**
   - Pi v1/UHG later docket resolved the function strip as Extract / Absorb / Elevate / Archive.
   - Unresolved material is represented through field truth, admissibility, verification, gaps/conflicts, recorded risk, and source archive—not a separate Quarantine command.

9. **Process revisions are source events.**
   - Grace startup processes are evolving.
   - Revised process material must be preserved, compared, and propagated with lineage instead of silently patched into the seed as if it had always been true.
