# Exchange Development Extraction v0.1

## Session

**Subject:** Pii Information Architecture Development

**Participants:**

- Human Designer (Sean Johnson)
- AI Design Collaborator (Hermes)

**Purpose**

Capture the reciprocal development of architectural ideas rather than only the resulting knowledge. Traditional extraction preserves conclusions; this preserves *how the architecture evolved* — who initiated a move, how the other party interpreted or extended it, what emerged, and what changed structurally as a result.

**Note on provenance:** This record captures the user's own narration of a prior exchange (produced in another session/context), pasted directly into this session for preservation. Hermes did not observe the underlying exchange first-hand; it is capturing the user's account of it, at the user's explicit instruction, as a new record type. This is disclosed here because the extraction's evidentiary chain runs through the user's summary, not through direct session logs.

## Exchange 005

### Initiation (Human)

> Phases are the basis of contractual workflow. They are not necessarily the workflow of the firm.

### AI Initial Interpretation

Recognized distinction between contracts and execution.

### Human Clarification

Operational workflow already exists. Contracts merely compartmentalize services.

### Emergent Insight

Three organizational structures emerged:

```text
Contract
  ↓
Operations
  ↓
Institutional Learning
```

### Architectural Consequence

Project phases were demoted from primary navigation to contextual metadata. This was one of the largest architectural changes in the session.

## Architectural Significance

This session represents a transition from knowledge management toward design intelligence. The principal realization was that preserving *how* ideas evolve between human and AI is as important as preserving the final ideas themselves.

The Chat Archive tells you what happened. The Extraction tells you what was learned. The Exchange Development Extraction tells Hermes how the architecture evolved — the artifact that lets a future AI collaborator join the design process with continuity instead of starting from static conclusions.

## Standing of this record

This is filed as a **candidate extraction sub-type**, not yet a ratified artifact class. Per Authority Rule 9 (this project's `AGENTS.md`) and the parent PI v1 constitution, an extraction — including a new extraction *type* — is preservation, not authority: it may be cited as source evidence but does not itself decide anything. Before "Exchange Development Extraction" is treated as a standing part of the schema (used routinely, referenced by tooling, expected by future agents), it should go through the same candidate → review → decision-record path as any other structural change:

1. This file stands as the first instance and de facto schema example.
2. A proposed decision record (`_governed/decisions/`, `status: candidate`) should formalize: what triggers capture, required frontmatter, where it's filed, and how it relates to existing `raw/` and `extractions/` conventions.
3. Human review promotes it (or amends/rejects it).

No further Exchange Development Extractions should be treated as routine practice until that decision record exists — this one is filed now, per explicit user instruction, because the source material was time-sensitive and at risk of degrading if deferred.
